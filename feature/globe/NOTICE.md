# Earth texture attribution

The bundled `textures/earth_night.jpg` is a reduced Earth-at-night texture derived from NASA Black Marble night-lights imagery.

Source imagery: NASA Black Marble / Earth at Night. Data and imagery credits include NASA/NOAA. The compact texture was sourced from the open-source dot-globe project, which identifies its embedded Earth-night texture as NASA Black Marble data.

NASA source information:
- NASA Earth at Night / Black Marble: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/
- NASA Scientific Visualization Studio Earth at Night: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2916

This asset is bundled locally so the native globe does not depend on a texture download at runtime.
